Injuries

For the rules about Injuries see Combat and Injuries.

Temporary Injuries

Bad Mood

Affects SPIRIT

You are in a bad mood, not exactely sad, not exactely angry ; the uncomfortable mixed of both. It negatively affects your Spirit aligned attributes.
(-2 on Charisam, Insight/Empathy and Volition)

A good night sleep should fix this.

System Details

Modifiy attributes : Charisma -2
Insight/Empathy -2
Volition -2

Blinded

Affects BODY

Something affected your sense of sight and you almost cannot see. You suffer from a -4d6 on your visions pools.
Thankfully it's temporary ; you will regain your sight after a while (at the GM discretion)

System Details

Modifiy senses : -4d6 on Vision in the sun -4d6 on Low light vision

Burnt Out

Affects SPIRIT

You gave it your all and more for too long. You are now in a state of constant exhaustion and stress. No matter how much you rest your exhaustion score and stress scores cannot go under 1.
You need at least 1 week of rest to heal from this injury. (Details on what rest exactely means for the caharacter to be determined by the GM and player together.)

System Details

Exhaution modified by 1
Stress modified by 1

Confused

Affects MIND

You cannot think straight. Too much information ; too much to process. It's hard to make the difference between what is important and what isn't. You are not sure you understand what is happening anymore.
-2 on all of your Mind aligned attributes.

System Details

Modifiy attributes : Memory/Knowledge -2
Wits -2
Composure -2

Deafen

Affects BODY

You have trouble hearing and suffer a -4d6 on your hearing dicepool.
Thankfully it's temporary ; you will regain your hearing after a while (at the GM discretion)

System Details

Modifiy senses : -4d6 on Hearing

Distracted

Affects MIND

It's harder to keep your focus.
Every activity, skill check, attack or spell that cost focus costs 1 more for you.

System Details

Forgetful

Affects MIND

You are in the kitchen, you know you had something to do there, but you forgot what. Checks using Memory have a difficulty of +5 for you.

System Details

Checks using Knowledge/Memory have a difficulty of +5

Out of Breath

Affects BODY

You are temporarily out of breath.
Every activity that cost endurance is more difficult. The difficulty of Body skills is at +5 for you, including Dodge and Resist checks.You need to rest your body for at least 10 min to get rid of this injury.

System Details

Difficulty of +5 on Body checks.

Panicked

Affects SPIRIT

You are panicking, your sympathetic autonomic nervous system is in full swing.
Your pupils dialte and your muscles are ready to react, but your cognitive functions are skewed. You are more likely to percieve situations or people in a negative light, assuming the worst.

System Details

Modifiy senses : +1d6 on Vision in the sun +1d6 on Low light vision
Modifiy attributes : Reaction Speed +2
Pain Tolerance +2
Memory/Knowledge -2
Wits -2
Composure -2

Rage

Affects SPIRIT

You are boiling with anger.

System Details

Modifiy skills : Intimidation +2
Persuasion -2
Fighting +2
Arcane Magic -2

Slow To Understand

System Details

Difficulty of +5 on Wits checks

Uncoordinated

Affects BODY

System Details

Undecided

Affects MIND

You have a hard time making simple decision. You might not be able to answer simple question such as "What do you want to eat tonight ? " or "Do you want it in red or blue ?"

System Details

Unmotivated

Affects SPIRIT

You really have no motivation to do anything right now...Every activity that cost focus, endurance or resilience cost an additional 1 resilience more.

System Details

Wounded

Affects BODY

You have a serious wound that needs healing and care. If you get an other injury before it is fully healed, this injury will turn into a permanent one.
(Decide alongside your storyteller the type of wound and how long it will need to fully heal)
oh, it also hurts like hell...

System Details

Permanent Injuries

Anorexia

Affects SPIRIT

Choose 1 :
Forgetful Anorexia : It's not Anorexia if you do not consciously stop yourself from eating, is it ?
You sometime forget to eat. At the end of the day roll 1d4 on an even number everything is as normal, on an odd number you forgot to eat or did not eat enough that day and your need to eat is not met for the day.

Control : Your need to eat is not completely satisfied, but you can choose to spend 2d4 focus or resilience and regain half as much endurance. (However, since your need to eat is not fulfilled, your exhaustion level will only be decremented by 1. see Recovery and Activities rules)

System Details

Anterograde Amnesia

Affects MIND

You are not able (or have great difficulties) to form new memories since you got this injury.

System Details

Blind

Affects BODY

You cannot use your Vision in the Sun and Low Light Vision dicepool.

System Details

Modifiy senses : -5d6 on Vision in the sun -5d6 on Low light vision

Bulimia

Affects SPIRIT

Choose 1 :
Every time you loose resilience, you must eat something or be unable to fulfil your Spirit needs. (You don't have to eat during a combat, but if you lost resilience during combat you must eat immediately after if possible.)

Your need to eat is double from what it was.

System Details

Chronic Pain

Affects BODY

Pain has become an unwanted travel compagnon.
Discuss with your GM how this injury affects your character.

System Details

Conspirasionist Mind

Affects MIND

Halflings are secretely manipulating every form of governement for their own interest.

Ancient Dragons are actually not extinct and will soon destroy the world.

Magic is a scam and is bad for your health !

You have found the secret formula to stop ageing based on a soap made from dwarven bread hair !

System Details

Crippled

Affects BODY

One part of your Body doesn't work anymore. You might be missing a leg or an arm.
Discuss with your GM how this injury affects your character.

System Details

Deaf

Affects BODY

You cannot use your Hearing dicepool.

System Details

Modifiy senses : -5d6 on Hearing

Depressed

Affects SPIRIT

You loose motivation and interest in what you used to like.
All Spirit checks have a Difficulty of +4 for you.
Every time you are about to engage in a Resourcing Activity roll 1d100 on a result higher than 50, you do not have enough energy to engage in the activity. You can add a bonus of +10 on the threshold for every friends that is wanting to do the activity with you. Additionally, if you manage to do the activity you still have to roll 1d100 on a result higher than 70, you only gain half of the benefits (rounded down, min of 1)

System Details

Checks using Spirit have a difficulty of +4

Feeble Minded

Affects MIND

Something almost completely broke your mind ; you still bear the scars of this invisible injury.
Your Knowledge and Wits attributes suffer from a -6.

System Details

Modifiy attributes : Memory/Knowledge -6
Wits -6

Mania

Affects SPIRIT

You feel so full of energy ! Nothing can stop you ! Every time you hit 0 endurance, you can regain 1d4 endurance without gainning any exhaustion.
However, if you roll a 1, you don't regain any endurance, but instead suffer 1d4 level of exhaustion and immediately suffer from the same amount of Body Injuries as normal.

Every time you hit 0 resilience, you can regain 1d4 resilience without gainning any level of stress.
However, if you roll a 1, you don't regain any resilience, but instead suffer 1d4 level of Stress and immediately suffer from the same amount of Spirit Injuries as normal.

System Details

Obsession

Affects MIND

You are obsess with something. It can be a goal you pursue, particullar object that you seek to acquire a person that you try to impress or emulate.

You gain a 1d4 bonus die on anything you do related directely to your goal, but any check not directely related to your goal has a difficulty of +5.

System Details

Difficulty of +5 on Checks not directly related to your goal.
