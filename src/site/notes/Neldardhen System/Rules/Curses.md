# Curses
Curses are permanent effects of magical origin that affects an individual greatly.
There sometime exists ways to lift a curse, but the ritual necessary is specific to the curse and must often be additionally tailored to the individual.
There are some curses for which there exist no known way to lift them.

## Permanent Curses

### Feral Dhampir
*Affects BODY*

You live with a constant hunger. This dormant Beast inside of you craves raw meat and blood. You can keep it under control, but for how long ?

Sometimes you feel that you are the Beast, chained in a prison of flesh.

> [!sys] System Details
> 
> 
> **Bestial Traits** : you cannot wear armour ; you barely stand your clothes, but it's ok, you have though skin and fur (**1d6** natural armour). 
> You don't use weapon either, you don't need them. You get the Claw slash and bite unarmed attack.
> 
> **Feral Dhampir Hunger** : you need raw meat... and you need to kill it yourself. At the end of the week, roll a **d6** for every week you went by without eating raw meat, if the result is greater than your current *focus* you loose control and go on a hunt.
> 
> **Sun Sensitivity** : if you spend too long in the sun and it will start to burn you. You take **1d4** *endurance* damage at the end of the day for every **2 hours** you spent in the sun.
> 
> **Unnatural Regeneration** : your body regenerates at an unnatural fast pace.
> You gain Fast Recovery (Body)
> 
> **Acute Senses** : you have very acute senses, but if you roll above a **20** on any sense, you are overwhelmed by sensations.
> You enter a frenzy if you have percieved a prey or you flee in panic if you have percieved a threat.
> You can stop your frenzy or panic from happening by spending **1d4** *focus*.
