# Injuries
For the rules about Injuries see [[Neldardhen System/Rules/Combat and Injuries#^b641f0\|Combat and Injuries]].
## Temporary Injuries

### Bad Mood
*Affects SPIRIT*

You are in a bad mood, not exactely sad, not exactely angry ; the uncomfortable mixed of both. It negatively affects your Spirit aligned attributes.
(-2 on Charisam, Insight/Empathy and Volition)

A good night sleep should fix this.

> [!sys] System Details
> Modifiy attributes : Charisma -2
> Insight/Empathy -2
> Volition -2
> 
> 
>
>
### Blinded
*Affects BODY*

Something affected your sense of sight and you almost cannot see. You suffer from a -4d6 on your visions pools.
Thankfully it's temporary ; you will regain your sight after a while (at the GM discretion)

> [!sys] System Details
> Modifiy senses :  -4d6 on Vision in the sun  -4d6 on Low light vision 
> 
>

### Burnt Out
*Affects SPIRIT*

You gave it your all and more for too long. You are now in a state of constant exhaustion and stress. No matter how much you rest your exhaustion score and stress scores cannot go under 1. 
You need at least 1 week of rest to heal from this injury. (Details on what rest exactely means for the caharacter to be determined by the GM and player together.)

> [!sys] System Details
> Exhaution modified by 1
> Stress modified by 1
> 
>
>

## Permanent Injuries

### Anorexia
*Affects SPIRIT*

Choose 1 :    
Forgetful Anorexia : It's not Anorexia if you do not consciously stop yourself from eating, is it ? 
You sometime forget to eat. At the end of the day roll 1d4 on an even number everything is as normal, on an odd number you forgot to eat or did not eat enough that day and your need to eat is not met for the day.    

Control :   Your need to eat is not completely satisfied, but you can choose to spend 2d4 focus or resilience and regain half as much endurance. (However, since your need to eat is not fulfilled, your exhaustion level will only be decremented by 1. see Recovery and Activities rules)

> [!sys] System Details
> 
>
>

### Depressed
*Affects SPIRIT*

You loose motivation and interest in what you used to like.
All Spirit checks have a Difficulty of +4 for you.
Every time you are about to engage in a Resourcing Activity roll 1d100 on a result higher than 50, you do not have enough energy to engage in the activity. You can add a bonus of +10 on the threshold for every friends that is wanting to do the activity with you. Additionally, if you manage to do the activity you still have to roll 1d100 on a result higher than 70, you only gain half of the benefits (rounded down, min of 1)

> [!sys] System Details
> 
>Checks using Spirit have a difficulty of +4
>

### Mania
*Affects SPIRIT*

You feel so full of energy ! Nothing can stop you ! Every time you hit 0 endurance, you can regain 1d4 endurance without gainning any exhaustion.
However, if you roll a 1, you don't regain any endurance, but instead suffer 1d4 level of exhaustion and immediately suffer from the same amount of Body Injuries as normal. 

Every time you hit 0 resilience, you can regain 1d4 resilience without gainning any level of stress.
However, if you roll a 1, you don't regain any resilience, but instead suffer 1d4 level of Stress and immediately suffer from the same amount of Spirit Injuries as normal.

> [!sys] System Details
> 
>
>
