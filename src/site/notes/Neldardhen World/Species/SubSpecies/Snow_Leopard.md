# Snow Leopard Caerfaldir
A Subspecies of [[Neldardhen World/Species/Caerfaldir\|Caerfaldir]] 



> [!sys] System Details
>
> **Snow Walker** : you can decide to sacrifice **4**m of movement to walk on all four and be able to walk on snow and ignore the effects of Extreme Terrain caused by snow. (Can also applies to effects similar to snow like ashes or sand to the Storyteller's discretion.)
> 
> **Greater Cold Resistance** : You gain a Resistance to Cold damage equals to your Body score.
>

### Level 3

#### A Cat Always Lands On Their Feets
**Prerequisites** : 
*Passive* 
When falling you can always try the Athletics checks to try and soften your landing. 
This checks has  **-4**  to its Difficulty for you. Additionally, you ignore any Critical Failure effect on such a check.

### Level 6

#### Soft Purr
**Prerequisites** : 
*At Will* 
If you and your group spend at leats **30**min doing nothing but relaxing, you can purr. The purring soothes you and those around you letting you and your allies regains **2** *resilience* points.
